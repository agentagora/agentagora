# Code of Conduct

AgentAgora follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want a project where people of any background can contribute and feel respected. Be helpful, be patient, give technical critique on the work and not the person. If something feels off, raise it.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@agentagora.dev**. Reports go to the maintainers and stay confidential. Include:

- What happened and where (issue / PR / Discussion link)
- Who was involved (handles are fine)
- Anything you'd like the response to look like

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond within 7 days. Outcomes range from a private nudge to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, scaled to the severity of the incident.

The full enforcement ladder is the one in the Contributor Covenant — we don't have anything custom yet.
